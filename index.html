Who we are

About Laar Youth Association (LYA)

Laar Youth Association is a youth-focused community initiative committed to positive social change, meaningful participation and stronger local communities.

We aim to bring young people together to identify local needs, develop practical solutions and create opportunities for people to learn, contribute and thrive.

  • Encourage education and personal development
  • Promote volunteerism and community welfare
  • Support environmental care and public awareness
  • Celebrate local culture, identity and heritage

What we do

Our Activities & Focus Areas

From learning and leadership to community service, LYA brings people together around practical actions for a better tomorrow.

📚

Education & Learning

Learning support, study guidance, reading initiatives, educational awareness and skills-building opportunities for young people.

🤝

Community Welfare

Volunteer-led community support, local welfare activities, helping vulnerable families and responding to identified needs.

🌟

Youth Leadership

Leadership sessions, teamwork, career awareness, confidence-building and positive opportunities for youth participation.

🌱

Environment & Cleanliness

Tree plantation, clean-up drives, waste awareness and community action to protect our shared environment.

📣

Social Awareness & Rights

Awareness campaigns on civic responsibility, equality, health, inclusion and issues affecting local communities.

🧵

Culture & Heritage

Celebrating local traditions, language, arts and cultural heritage while encouraging respect and intergenerational connection.

💚

Health & Wellbeing Awareness

Community awareness, healthy-living information and collaboration around wellbeing and access to useful resources.

💻

Digital & Career Skills

Promoting digital literacy, career information, communication skills and access to learning resources.

🗣️

Community Dialogue

Creating constructive spaces for young people and residents to discuss challenges, share ideas and work together.
